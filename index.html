01 / Rec Room · Visual scripting · Solo project

How Far Can You Ramp?

906.4K visits · Solo design and implementation

A driving simulator with a repeatable progression loop: drive, launch, earn based on landing distance, and spend on Ramp Speed upgrades to travel farther.

Gameplay systems

Progression: Landing distance determines currency earnings; players spend earnings on Ramp Speed upgrades to extend the next run.

Rebirth and persistence: At maximum speed, players can reset spendable currency and upgrades in exchange for a permanent +1 landing earnings multiplier while retaining lifetime statistics.

Random rewards: Golden Eggs award randomized Gold amounts across rarity tiers, communicated through color, sound, and reward values.

Tuning and investigation

Economy tuning: Researched simulator pricing, adjusted upgrade costs and reward odds, and tuned the mapping between an internal speed variable and the player facing display.

Bug investigation: After vehicles disrupted players near spawn, I added a restricted area respawn trigger and an explanation.

Measurement limitation: No before and after incident rate was recorded for the spawn fix.

02 / Rec Room · Visual scripting · two person team

Big Hands

840.6K visits · Gameplay systems and interaction design

An asymmetric multiplayer experience where VR players use giant hands to pick up screen players, while screen players can escape, hide, drive and interact with the environment.

Interaction systems

Asymmetric roles: VR players grab and carry screen players; screen players can escape, use cars, hide or unlock keys that prevent them from being picked up.

Interactive experiences: Built logic around planet journeys, a freezer that turns players to ice, a timed microwave, and a cannon that launches players toward a target.

Lifecycle: Screen players respawn after experiences to rejoin the interaction loop.

Iteration and contribution

Usability fix: Originally, letting go of a giant hand made it respawn immediately. After feedback, I delayed the respawn so VR players could use it more conveniently.

Effects: A nuke interaction combined visual effects and sound and became the game's best selling item.

Team role: I designed and implemented gameplay and interaction logic; my teammate sourced and placed environment assets.

Quality assurance

Finding bugs. Testing fixes.

Hands on multiplayer testing, bug investigation and verification from games I developed. I tested with real players during beta events and live sessions.

01 / Multiplayer bug / How Far Can You Ramp?

Missing vehicles after launch

Problem: Some cars failed to return to spawn after a run, leaving fewer cars available.

Expected: Empty cars return automatically. Cars with seated players stay in play.

Fix: Built a timed trigger volume check that detected vehicles and checked seat occupancy before returning unoccupied cars to spawn.

Verification: Tested with players in an active session, moved the working logic to the main server and played for hours afterward without observing the issue again.

02 / Gameplay bug / How Far Can You Ramp?

Vehicle collisions at spawn

Problem: Cars could hit players, knock them away and leave them stuck in walls.

Fix: Added a restricted area trigger to respawn vehicles entering the player spawn zone.

Impact: Addressed a disruption identified during multiplayer playtesting.
