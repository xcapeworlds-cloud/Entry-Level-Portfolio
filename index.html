Test case example

Sticker placement and snapping

Project: Sticker Puzzles: Match Scenes

Purpose: Check whether stickers snap into the intended position.

Test steps:

  1. Open a puzzle with draggable stickers.
  2. Drag a sticker toward its matching position.
  3. Release it near the correct placement area.
  4. Repeat with different release positions.

Expected: A correctly placed sticker snaps into position.

What I worked on: I improved sticker placement by adding snapping behavior after playtesting revealed that placement could feel imprecise.
